About

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Fondazione 1563 per l'Arte e la Cultura in Turin, working on the intellectual history of the 20th-century United States. Previously, I was a Lecturer in US History at the UCL Institute of the Americas, having completed my PhD at the University of Sussex.

Current Research

My current research examines the evolution of totalitarian theory and transatlantic intellectual networks in the mid-to-late twentieth century, focusing on how ideas of democracy and authoritarianism were debated and reformulated.

Selected Writing

  • "Norman Mailer and Totalitarianism," Modern Intellectual History.
  • Contributions to Journal of the History of Ideas Blog and H-Diplo.
